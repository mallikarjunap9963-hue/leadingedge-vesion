8 Days of Discovery, STEM & Inspiration

Inspiring Future Innovators Through Science & Technology — An 8-day experiential STEM trip to Japan.

DAY 1

Arrival in Tokyo

SENSOJI TEMPLE — ASAKUSA

Tokyo's oldest and most iconic Buddhist temple. Explore Nakamise-dori, the traditional shopping street, and discover the cultural heart of old Tokyo.

AKIHABARA — ELECTRONICS CITY

The world-famous electronics district. Witness the latest tech gadgets, gaming culture and the bleeding edge of Japanese consumer innovation.

📍 Sensoji Temple — Asakusa
📍 Akihabara — Electronics City
DAY 2

Space & Science Exploration

JAXA — JAPAN AEROSPACE EXPLORATION AGENCY

Tour Japan's premier space agency. See actual rockets, satellites, and learn about Japan's ambitious space exploration programs firsthand.

TSUKUBA SCIENCE CITY

Visit one of the world's foremost research clusters, home to 300+ research institutes, universities, and innovators shaping the future of science.

📍 JAXA — Space Agency
📍 Tsukuba Science City
DAY 3

Innovation & City Views

MIRAIKAN — NATIONAL MUSEUM OF EMERGING SCIENCE

Japan's premier science museum featuring interactive AI, robotics, space exploration exhibits and the iconic Geo-Cosmos digital globe.

TOKYO TOWER OBSERVATORY

Panoramic views of the Tokyo skyline from the iconic 333m tower. A symbol of Japan's post-war technological renaissance and national pride.

📍 Miraikan Museum
📍 Tokyo Tower Observatory
DAY 4

Robotics & Future Innovation

KAWASAKI ROBOT MUSEUM

Hands-on encounters with industrial and humanoid robots. See Kawasaki's duAro collaborative robot and discover how automation is reshaping manufacturing.

HANEDA INNOVATION CITY

A cutting-edge smart city complex at Haneda Airport. Experience Japan's future through tech startups, research labs and immersive innovation showcases.

📍 Kawasaki Robot Museum
📍 Haneda Innovation City
DAY 5

Mount Fuji & Scenic Hakone

MOUNT FUJI — 富士山 (3,776m)

Marvel at Japan's sacred and iconic peak framed by cherry blossoms. Explore the Fuji Five Lakes region and experience Japan's most beloved natural wonder.

LAKE ASHI — PIRATE CRUISE, HAKONE

Sail Lake Ashi aboard a replica galleon with spectacular views of Mt. Fuji on clear days. A unique and unforgettable experience in the Hakone region.

📍 Mount Fuji — 富士山
📍 Lake Ashi — Pirate Cruise
DAY 6

School Exchange & Robot Restaurant

TOKYO SCHOOL CULTURAL EXCHANGE

Interact with Japanese students in a meaningful cross-cultural exchange. Share ideas, compare education systems and build global friendships that last a lifetime.

ROBOT RESTAURANT EXPERIENCE

Dine surrounded by cutting-edge robotic technology in an unforgettable futuristic experience. A spectacular fusion of entertainment and innovation unique to Japan.

📍 Tokyo School Exchange
📍 Robot Restaurant
DAY 7

Tokyo Disneyland — A Day of Magic & Imagination

A full day at one of the world's most celebrated theme parks — the perfect reward after a week of scientific discovery and cultural exploration.

📍 Tokyo Disneyland
DAY 8

Departure Back to India

Departure back to India with lifelong memories, enhanced scientific knowledge, and global perspectives.

📍 Tokyo Haneda Airport
