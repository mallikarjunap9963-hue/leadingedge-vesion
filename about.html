ABOUT LEADING EDGE VISION

From Electronics to
Semiconductor Technology

Leading Edge Vision Private Limited, earlier known as Vision Mass Communications Private Limited, was established in 1993 with a pioneering focus on manufacturing Video Titlers under the PAN-India-renowned VMC Video Titlers brand, which was well known in the Indian Cable Television industry.

Over the years, the company has evolved from its strong foundation in electronics and communication technology into a diversified technology-driven organization with a focus on Semiconductor Manufacturing Technology, Semiconductor Laboratories, Science, Technology and Education.

JAPAN TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE STUDY TOURS

Our Japan Technology and Science Study Tours have been a major success, providing students from leading schools and colleges across India with opportunities to experience advanced technology, innovation and industrial practices firsthand.

The programmes are designed to go beyond classroom learning by providing students with exposure to Japan's technology ecosystem, research institutions, industries, advanced manufacturing facilities and emerging technologies.

Through these initiatives, we aim to create an enriching learning experience that connects academic knowledge with real-world applications.

OUR VISION

To contribute to India's technological advancement by connecting education, innovation, industry and advanced technology, while creating meaningful opportunities for students and institutions to experience global technological developments.
