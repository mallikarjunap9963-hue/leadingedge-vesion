ABOUT RAJ GROUP

  • We are established in Tokyo, Japan since 1998 operating six time award winning restaurant chain Nirvanam with total 9 restaurants in Tokyo under Rajgroup Pvt, Ltd.,

  • Apart from restaurants we also operate International Schools, Software company, Language school, Consultancy, Robotics and STEM tour for school students etc. at Tokyo, Japan

  • We are proud to say that we are the First company from India to place construction works at Japan under National Skill Development Council (NSDC). Our partner Mr. MV Anil Raj is an official advisor to NSDCI for Japan.
